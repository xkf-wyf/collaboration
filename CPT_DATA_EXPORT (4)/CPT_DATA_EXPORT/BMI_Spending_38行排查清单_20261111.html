三、逐类根因的接口证据

C1 store_name 归并不对称  ·  7 行 / 337,480 元 / 60.0%

接口侧（ads_bmi_spending_mf.sql 第 131 行，20260904 起）：

case when customer = 'JD - REGENERATE' then 'JD - UL' else customer end as store_name

校验侧（RTB 对数的 t2 子查询，20260907 改动）：

-- ,case when store_group='TMALL SUPER' then 'TMALL SUPER - UL'
--       --  when store_group='JD - REGENERATE' then 'JD - UL'
-- else store_group end as store_name --20260421修改
,store_group  as store_name --20260907          ← 归并被注释

后果链

  • t1 的 JD - UL = UL + REGENERATE（接口已归并）
  • t2 的 JD - UL = 仅 UL；REGENERATE 单独成行
  • where coalesce(t1.store_name,t2.store_name) in ($store) 的白名单里没有 JD - REGENERATE → t2 侧那行被过滤掉
  • ⇒ JD - UL 凭空出现「CPT 多算」，金额 = REGENERATE 的 RTB 花费

7 行全部是 JD - UL，方向全部为 CPT > golden，gap% 在 0.43%–3.14% 之间浮动 —— 与「REGENERATE 自身体量随月波动」的形态一致。此项为校验口径缺陷，非数据异常。

C2 五键组合在 golden 源侧不存在  ·  14 行 / 161,561 元 / 28.7%

14 行的 golden = 0，意味着 ads_ec_g_fomular_ndy_traffic_fact 里根本没有这个 (month, store_group, category, brand, product_group) 组合。拆分如下：

202609 的 DEO / LUX44,265PDD-UL(2)、JD-UL(2)
202601 的 PW / DOVE / OTHERS11,000PDD-UL

关键点：202610 的 DEO / LUX 跨 5 个不同渠道的店铺同时归零，单店归店口径解释不了，指向黄金公式侧该品牌在该月整体缺失。

待确认：是「品牌级」（仅 LUX 缺，DOVE / REXONA 正常）还是「品类级」（整个 DEO 缺）。这决定向上游提单的写法。

C4 category / brand 打标来源不同  ·  12 行 / 46,535 元 / 8.3%

接口侧：category / brand / bu 有两套来源 ——

  • 分支①（item 命中打标表）：dim_prd_item.category / brand / bu，按 period_key between start_time and end_time 落版本区间
  • 分支②（店铺维度，item_id in ('0','-') 或 Package 无 item）：pfm_category / pfm_brand / pfm_bu

校验侧：fact.category / brand / bu（事实表自带列）

两套打标若在某批 item 上不一致，金额就会落在不同的 (category, brand) 组合上。表现为持续性小额比例差：VASELINE/LIP&CUTTIE 10 个月全在（0.35%–2.86%）、HHC/CIF 比例高但品类小（16%–38%）。

四、验证 SQL（全部使用校验脚本自己的源表）

V1　验证 C1：REGENERATE 是否造成了 JD - UL 的 337,480

select replace(period,'-','')                as month
     , store_group
     , case when store_group = 'JD - REGENERATE' then 'JD - UL'
            else store_group end             as store_name_merged
     , round(sum(value), 2)                  as golden_value
from ${spark.var.catalog.ads}.ddm_dset.ads_ec_g_fomular_ndy_traffic_fact t1
left join ${spark.var.catalog.ads}.ddm_dset.ads_ec_g_fomular_dim_traffic_metric_cfg t2
       on t1.metric_id = t2.metric_id
where period_type = 'Month'
  and period between '2026-01' and '2026-10'
  and t2.column_name = 'rtb_spending'
  and store_group like 'JD%'
  and category = 'HAIR'
  and brand    = 'CLEAR'
  and product_group = 'CORE'
group by replace(period,'-',''), store_group
order by month, store_group;

判定：若出现 JD - REGENERATE 行，且各月金额与 C1 的 gap 对得上 → C1 定案（数据本身没问题，是校验归并口径漏改）。

V4　量化 C5：清理所有 int() 假阳性

select *
from (
    select coalesce(t1.month, t2.month)                 as month
         , coalesce(t1.store_name, t2.store_name)       as store_name
         , coalesce(t1.category, t2.category)           as category
         , coalesce(t1.brand, t2.brand)                 as brand
         , coalesce(t1.product_line_group, t2.product_line_group) as plg
         , round(coalesce(rtb_spending_1,0) - coalesce(rtb_spending_2,0), 2) as real_gap
    from (
        select month, store_name, category_name as category, brand_name as brand
             , product_line_group, round(sum(value_taxed),2) as rtb_spending_1
        from ${spark.var.catalog.ads}.fin_cpt.ads_bmi_spending_mf
        where dt = '$dt' and index_type = 'Paid Media-RTB'
        group by month, store_name, category_name, brand_name, product_line_group
    ) t1
    full join (
        select replace(period,'-','') as month, store_group as store_name
             , category, brand, product_group as product_line_group
             , sum(value) as rtb_spending_2
        from ${spark.var.catalog.ads}.ddm_dset.ads_ec_g_fomular_ndy_traffic_fact a
        left join ${spark.var.catalog.ads}.ddm_dset.ads_ec_g_fomular_dim_traffic_metric_cfg b
               on a.metric_id = b.metric_id
        where period_type = 'Month' and period between '$start_mth' and '$end_mth'
          and b.column_name = 'rtb_spending'
        group by replace(period,'-',''), store_group, category, brand, product_group
    ) t2
      on t1.month = t2.month and t1.store_name = t2.store_name
     and t1.category = t2.category and t1.brand = t2.brand
     and coalesce(t1.product_line_group,'<NULL>') = coalesce(t2.product_line_group,'<NULL>')
) t
where real_gap <> 0 and abs(real_gap) < 1
order by abs(real_gap) desc;

用途：估算「待查」里有多少是 1 元以下的噪音。若结果集基本覆盖待查清单，说明剩余差异整体已低于 1 元量级。
